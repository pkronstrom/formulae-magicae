# Enclosure and mechanical fit

For scripted solid modeling, STEP/STL export and headless renders, use the [CadQuery Compose service](hardware-containers.md#enclosure-cad) when the project selects that backend.

For concept work, use a brief, assumed envelope and desired handling. Before accepting a populated-board fit, use the routed board at a known coordinate origin, mounted component heights, keepouts/clearances, fasteners, assembly access and the selected manufacturing process. Join this work early during arrangement. For an existing case such as a mint tin, measure the usable inside at board height and with the lid closed, including rounded corners, hinges, lip and insertion path. Establish retention, isolation where applicable and allowable modifications before fixing the outline or ports.

Use an editable parametric source and repeatable exports. If the user has `cad-studio`, reuse its relevant modeling and review procedures. Choose OpenSCAD, FreeCAD, build123d or another available backend by its ability to preserve source, exchange current geometry, measure fit and regenerate previews. Keep geometry in the gadget repo and mark measured, inferred and assumed dimensions. FDM, outsourced plastics and metal printing keep separate process assumptions and variant evidence.

An optional user starter can show the PCB and critical components as a fixed reference. With JumpStart 3D or a similar tool, verify dimensions/orientation and editable export in the user's browser before relying on the handoff. Its offline HTML may be run separately from the board. A dimensioned sketch with top/side views also works. Preserve the user's source, PCB revision and what they want kept or changed. Use the real part geometry to finish the design and send the next view to the board.

## Model the assembly and check worst-case fit

1. **Create a dimensioned board assembly.** Export the current board and installed parts as STEP for CAD when available. Use it as a fixed reference at the agreed board origin. Check the outline, thickness, hole centers, and installed position/orientation of parts that affect fit. Replace missing models with simple solids sized from drawings or measurements. Include protruding pins, relevant solder or connector features, and parts on both sides. Record the source of each critical dimension. An assumed dimension can support a draft; leave the corresponding fit check open until the dimension is established. Use the arrangement revision for early work and refresh the reference from the release candidate for final checks. [KiCad 3D model export](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html#_3d_models_step_glb_brep_xao_ply_stl_stpz_u3d_pdf)
2. **Build the case around clearances as well as parts.** Use the largest expected assembly and smallest expected cavity for each mating feature. Include board, part and placement variation and the selected process/material tolerances, including finishing if it changes fit. Check that the worst-case remaining gap meets the intended assembly clearance. Model the space needed for insertion and removal, cable or plug engagement, button travel, screw access and the lid. For locating features, check alignment tolerance rather than comparing overall dimensions alone. [Example printing tolerances and clearance guidance](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/3d-printing-design-guideline)
3. **Show and record the limiting fits.** Run section, gap or interference checks in CAD as supported. Keep a short record of the dimensions and tolerances used, minimum remaining clearance, and any missing evidence. Show the tightest places in the board preview. Where an unverified fit has little margin, propose a first article or a small print of the mating features. Record any physical result separately from the CAD check.

**Capture `enclosure`.** Output roles `mechanical` (editable CAD or measured existing-case model/record) and `visual` (dimensioned section and assembly view, combined if useful). Add exact geometry, parts and settings via `--uses`. The `fit` check covers tolerances, mounting, protected component volumes, lid and insertion path, opening alignment and applicable process limits. Report physically untested properties. A question affecting board shape/placement goes back to arrangement and routing before accepting another revision.
