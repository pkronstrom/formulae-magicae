Latin Modern — licence
======================

`template.html` embeds four Latin Modern faces as base64 `@font-face` rules
(regular, italic, bold, bold-italic), so a rendered visualization keeps its
typography offline and off a CDN. The faces are redistributed verbatim; nothing
is derived or renamed, so the renaming clause below does not apply.

Latin Modern is produced by GUST (the Polish TeX Users Group) and released under
the GUST Font License, reproduced in full below. Upstream copy:
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt

The same faces back the `latex` preset in the `bento` skill. There is one copy in
this repository and one licence — this file.
