# Fastening vocabulary (Finland) — names, not engineering

Words the joints and hardware tables use so a hardware-store list reads naturally. Sizing
is general practice to be verified; the booklet's disclaimer covers it.

| Finnish | English | typical use in a small timber build |
|---|---|---|
| kulmarauta | angle bracket | rafter foot / post to rim; 90×90 or 105×105, nailed with ankkurinaula |
| ankkurinaula | connector nail | 4.0×40 into brackets and joist hangers |
| palkkikenkä | joist hanger | joist into rim or beam |
| pultti + mutteri | bolt + nut | M10/M12, ties and collar ties to rafters |
| prikka / aluslevy | washer | under every bolt head and nut in timber; large (Ø 30+) for softwood |
| kierretanko | threaded rod | anchors in pier cores |
| ruuvi (yleisruuvi) | wood screw | 4×40… 6×120; TX head; framing and sheathing |
| terassiruuvi | deck screw | stainless/A2, 4.8×75 or 5×80 for decking |
| kansiruuvi / kuusiokoloruuvi | coach screw | 8×80… 10×160 where a bolt can't go through |
| hakanen / niitti | staple | tervapahvi, aluskate, tuulensuojapaperi |
| kattoruuvi | roofing screw | 4.8×35 self-drilling with EPDM washer, pelti to ruoteet |
| naula (kuumasinkitty) | galvanised nail | 75–100 mm, harvalaudoitus, battens |
| liima (PU / PVAc D3) | glue | plywood gussets, glued + screwed both faces |
| teippi (höyrynsulkuteippi) | vapour-barrier tape | overlaps and penetrations of the vapour barrier; tervapahvi seams against wind |

Direction words for the how-to column: läpi (through), sivusta (from the side),
molemmin puolin (both faces), viistoon (skewed / toe), päältä (from above).

Tools: räikkä + 19 mm hylsy (M12), TX25/TX30 bits, 12 mm auger for M12 through-bolts.
